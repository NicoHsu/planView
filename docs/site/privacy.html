Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

PlanView is a local-first productivity app. It does not require an account or sync your data to a PlanView server.

Data stored on your device

Task titles, tags, time estimates, session start and end times, completion outcomes, reflection notes, reasons for incomplete sessions, and app preferences are stored locally. They provide your task pool, history, and time-estimate insights.

Notifications

PlanView requests optional notification permission to remind you when a focus block ends. Notifications are scheduled on your device. If you deny permission, the timer still works and is recalculated when you reopen the app.

Collection and sharing

PlanView does not upload your tasks or history to its servers, sell personal data, use advertising trackers, include third-party analytics, or track you across apps or websites.

Deleting data

Deleting the app removes its local data according to your operating system's app deletion behavior. Device backups may retain data according to your device and backup settings.

This website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information to operate hosting; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site does not use analytics scripts or advertising cookies.

Children and health

PlanView is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or therapy.
