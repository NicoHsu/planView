# PlanView Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-29

PlanView is designed as a local-first productivity app. The first App Store version does not require an account and does not sync data to a server.

## Data Stored On Device

PlanView stores the following data locally on your device:

- Task titles.
- Tags.
- Estimated focus duration.
- Session start and end times.
- Completion status.
- Reflection notes.
- Reasons for incomplete or blocked sessions.
- Local app preferences.

This data is used to provide history, tag summaries, and Plan vs Actual insights.

## Notifications

PlanView asks for notification permission so it can remind you when a focus block ends.

If you deny notification permission, the app still works. PlanView recalculates timer status when you reopen the app.

## Data Collection

The first version of PlanView does not:

- Require user registration.
- Upload tasks, tags, or session history to PlanView servers.
- Sell personal data.
- Use third-party advertising trackers.
- Track users across apps or websites.

## Analytics

The first version does not include third-party analytics.

If analytics are added in the future, this policy should be updated before release.

## Data Deletion

Because the app stores data locally, deleting the app from your device removes local PlanView data according to iOS app deletion behavior.

## Children

PlanView is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children.

## Health And Medical Disclaimer

PlanView is not a medical app. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, therapy, or medical advice.
